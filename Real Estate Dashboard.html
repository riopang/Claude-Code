Talking points for clients
  • OCR momentum is real: prices +2.2%, rents +1.0%, vacancy lowest at 5.2% — supports the Lucerne Grand / JLD thesis and HDB-upgrader conversations.
  • CCR recovering but watch vacancy: swung −3.5% → +0.6%, yet vacancy is highest at 8.2%. Build vacancy buffer into CCR rental-yield projections (Dunearn House, UPPERHOUSE, Promenade Peak).
  • Demand is selective, not euphoric: Apr's 1,548 sales were concentrated in location+value launches (Tengah 99%, Vela Bay 72%). Position pipeline projects on fundamentals, not FOMO.
  • Buyers are local: 88.9% Singaporean, 9.6% PR, 1.5% foreigner (Apr 2026) — ABSD-sensitive structuring (decoupling, 1st-property timing) matters more than ever.
