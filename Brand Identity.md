## Unique Value Proposition
Rio Pang is not just a transactor — he is a trusted advisor who explains the *why* behind every decision. Whether you are decoding ABSD implications, timing your HDB sale, or evaluating a new launch against resale value, Rio gives you clarity so you can act with confidence.

---

## Tone of Voice

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Warm, conversational, like a knowledgeable friend | Cold, transactional, jargon-heavy |
| Confident but never arrogant | Oversell or use pressure tactics |
| Educate first, sell second | Push listings without context |
| Speak in specifics (PSF, D20, $xx psf) | Vague claims ("best agent in SG") |
| Use Singlish sparingly for relatability | Overdo it to the point it seems unauthentic |

**Sample phrase in brand voice:**
> *"A lot of buyers ask me — should I buy now or wait? Honestly, it depends on your holding power and your why. Let me break it down for you."*
